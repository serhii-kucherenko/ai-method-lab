# PRODUCT — Contact Arm Studio

## Buyer outcome

Robotics teams plan intended arm contact, attach tactile and vision cues, and understand whether contact-centric evidence changes a vision-only decision before a soft-sim handoff.

## Surfaces

- Manipulator pack registry (`/manipulators`)
- Contact-point editor (`/contacts`)
- Contact-plan workspace (`/plans`)
- Tactile+vision sensing runs (`/sensing`)
- Contact-centric vs vision-only compare + scoreboard
- Pricing, demo, onboarding, multi-flow index, honesty fence, settings

## Platform must-haves

Bearer auth, org/members, webhook HMAC, audit, export, search, pagination, rate limit, ≥30 dual goldens, `try.html`.
