# Hypothesis

Computational chemistry / platform leads will prefer locking a workflow pack after seeing a soft-sim A/B gap between typed trace-state validated agentic workflows and ungated agent baselines — especially when ASP allow/deny transitions and recovery configs are explicit.

## Falsifiers

- Buyers treat soft-sim scores as certified compliance or live HPC readiness.
- Ungated baselines always dominate once skip-optimism is high, making typed-trace packs unused.
- Trace rules without recovery clarity do not change lock decisions.
