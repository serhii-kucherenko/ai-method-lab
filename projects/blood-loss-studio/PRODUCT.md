# Blood Loss Studio — product brief

**Buyer:** Obstetric / perinatal analytics leads  
**Outcome:** Compare weighed-swab measured blood loss vs haemoglobin-calculated baselines; lock when measured wins with soft-sim honesty.

## Surfaces

Landing, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, packs, births, methods, assays, compare, scoreboard, settings.

## Platform

Auth (bearer), org/members, audit, export, webhook (HMAC + idempotency), search, pagination, rate limit.

## Claim fence

Soft-sim only — not clinical advice, EMR write-back, or device clearance.
