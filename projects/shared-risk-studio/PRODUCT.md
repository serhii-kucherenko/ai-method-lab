# Product — Shared Risk Studio

**Buyer:** Population health / biobank ML eng leads  
**Outcome:** Version cohort packs, modality schemas, and risk queries; compare shared multi-disease representations vs disease-specific baselines; lock when deltas and honesty are understood.  
**Stack:** Next.js App Router + Tailwind + shadcn  
**Paper:** https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.07.07.26357373v1
