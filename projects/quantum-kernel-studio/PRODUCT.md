# PRODUCT — Quantum Kernel Studio

## Buyer outcome

Chem-informatics leads manage molecule packs, quantum multiple-kernel configs, and binding targets, then understand whether a quantum multi-kernel QSAR plan beats a classical kernel baseline before a soft-sim handoff.

## Surfaces

- Molecule pack registry (`/molecules`)
- Kernel config editor (`/kernels`)
- Target case workspace (`/targets`)
- QSAR soft-sim runs (`/runs`)
- Quantum vs classical compare + scoreboard
- Pricing, demo, onboarding, multi-flow index, honesty fence, settings

## Platform must-haves

Bearer auth, org/members, webhook HMAC, audit, export, search, pagination, rate limit, ≥30 dual goldens, `try.html`.
