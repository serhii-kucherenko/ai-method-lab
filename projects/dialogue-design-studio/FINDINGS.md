# Findings — Dialogue Design Studio

## What shipped
Soft-sim feed packs with dual scoring: productive open-minded design vs engagement-maximizing baseline. Domain IA uses packs/feeds/badges/topics/runs — not jobs/lifecycle/scenario desks.

## Scorers
- `productive_open_minded_design` rewards open-mindedness, badge clarity, topic balance, pack readiness
- `engagement_maximizing_baseline` rewards engagement pull and outrage tunnel

## Honesty
Method-lab only. No live social network deployment, moderation authority, attitude-change clearance, or authors’ platform brand claims.

## Source
SocArXiv preprint dngcj_v3 — Designing Social Media to Promote Productive Political Dialogue. Authors’ code: none.
