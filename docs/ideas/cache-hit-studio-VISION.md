# Vision — Cache Hit Studio

## Problem
Discovery teams crow about dock scores without a prospective hit-finding baseline. They lack a soft-sim bench that compares structured CACHE-style packs to naive docking before locking a hit pack.

## Buyer

## Promise
Version hit packs, configure targets and compounds, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not an accel-pd or assay-guard desk. Domain nouns: hits, targets, compounds, runs, compares.
