Soft-sim studio where NICU analytics leads compare alignment-free PPG-guided ECG inpainting to alignment-dependent baselines.

Ecg pack CRUD, ppg, inpaints, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Clinical diagnostic claims, live device write-back, FDA clearance, paper brand.
