# Design — Aminoaryl Studio

## Brand
**Aminoaryl Studio** — soft-sim for photocatalytic vs copper-catalyzed 1,3-aminoarylation route packs. Never claim wet-lab validation, scale-up manufacturing control, or regulatory filing authority.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Light or copper. Compare the route.
- **Support:** Route packs for 1,3-aminoarylation — compare photocatalytic paths against copper-catalyzed baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not wet-lab validated; not manufacturing control; not regulatory filing; not authors’ brand
- Sources: ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15005923/v1 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** flask ink + photo teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--aa-ink: #14181c`, `--aa-teal: #1a5c66`, `--aa-mist: #eef3f4`, `--aa-line: #c5cbce`, `--aa-amber: #a67a30`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft mist-field atmosphere
