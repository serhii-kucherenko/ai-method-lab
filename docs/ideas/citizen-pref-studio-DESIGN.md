# Design — Citizen Pref Studio

## Brand
**Citizen Pref Studio** — soft-sim for safety-first public-oversight AI regulation preference packs. Never claim live regulatory authority, government deployment, or certified public-opinion polling.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Build the policy pack the public actually ranks higher.
- **Support:** Policy packs for AI governance — compare safety-first public oversight against innovation-first self-regulation before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live regulatory authority; not government deployment; not certified polling; not authors’ survey brand
- Sources: arXiv 2607.14585 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** civic ink + preference teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--cp-ink: #151a22`, `--cp-teal: #2a6670`, `--cp-mist: #eef2f5`, `--cp-line: #c3c9ce`, `--cp-amber: #b8863a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft civic-preference mist
