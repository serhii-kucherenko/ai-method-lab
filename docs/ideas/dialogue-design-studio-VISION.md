# Vision — Dialogue Design Studio

## Problem
Engagement-maximizing feed designs reward outrage and collapse productive dialogue. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares open-minded dialogue designs to engagement-maximizing baselines before locking a feed pack.

## Buyer
Civic-tech / platform trust-and-safety / deliberative media eng leads.

## Promise
Version feed packs, configure badges and topics, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a citizen-pref or surveil-gate desk. Domain nouns: packs, feeds, badges, topics, runs, compares.
