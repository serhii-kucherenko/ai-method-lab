# PRD — Phe Escalate Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where public-health ops leads compare AI-assisted outbreak escalation to manual triage baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Escalate pack CRUD, classifications, thresholds, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Operational MoH authority, live write-back, clinical diagnosis, paper brand.
