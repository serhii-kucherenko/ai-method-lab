# Design — Margin Fusion Studio

## Brand
**Margin Fusion Studio** — soft-sim for marker-free AR margin fusion. Never claim surgical device clearance or live OR guidance.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Map the margin without the markers.
- **Support:** Case packs with deformable fusion — compare against marker-based baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open cases → `/cases`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not surgical device cleared; not live OR; not authors’ system
- Sources: arXiv 2607.13343 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** OR slate + sterile cyan + warm gauze (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--mf-ink: #1a2228`, `--mf-cyan: #2a8f9e`, `--mf-gauze: #eef2f3`, `--mf-line: #c3ced1`, `--mf-rose: #b85a62`
- **Typography:** Plus Jakarta Sans (display) + Source Sans 3 (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft gauze mist + faint fusion-grid
