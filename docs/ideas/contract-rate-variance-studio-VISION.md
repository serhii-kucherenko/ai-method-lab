# Contract Rate Variance Studio — Vision

Vendor invoices quietly drift off the contracted unit rate until AP pays or Finance disputes late. Contract Rate Variance Studio soft-sims a **contract catalog matched to invoice lines** versus an invoice-as-billed baseline so AP and FinOps leads open payment runs with a dollar variance queue, not another OCR inbox.

**Refuse to become:** invoice OCR theater, ERP three-way-match clone, True Up (seats), Commitment Coverage (cloud commits), or desk jobs/lifecycle shell.
