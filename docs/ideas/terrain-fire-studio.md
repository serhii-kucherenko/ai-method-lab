## Buyer
Wildfire GIS and land-management planning leads who must refresh 3D terrain with new aerial photos before crews trust outdated ground.

## Outcome
A studio to manage terrain packs, aerial refreshes, alignment plans, compare physics-aware refresh vs naive overlay baselines, plus pricing, demo, onboarding, and ≥5 named user flows with category platform must-haves.

## Why this paper
LTM claims large-scale terrain modeling for wildfire-prone landscapes by aligning new aerial photos to older 3D maps. Industrial / public-safety software claim — preferred under biz-rubric-v1. Distinct from Sign Stream (a11y) and Hold Match (ride-hail). Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an LTM rebrand.
