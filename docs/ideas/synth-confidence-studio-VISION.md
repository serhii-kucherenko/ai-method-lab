# Vision — Synth Confidence Studio

## Problem
Naive AI retrosynthesis proposes flashy but untrusted routes. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares confidence-gated AI routes to naive AI baselines before locking a route pack.

## Buyer
Process chemistry / route-design eng leads.

## Promise
Version route packs, configure retrosynthesis and confidence scores, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a retro-route or reaction-loop desk. Domain nouns: routes, scores, candidates, runs, compares.
