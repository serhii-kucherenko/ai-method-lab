# Vision — Probe Domain Studio

## Problem
Single-domain melting probes struggle under physiological noise and incomplete targets. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares cooperative multi-domain probe designs to single-domain baselines before locking a probe pack.

## Buyer
Molecular diagnostics / assay-design analytics eng leads.

## Promise
Version probe packs, configure domains and targets, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a dialogue-design or crate-validate desk. Domain nouns: packs, probes, domains, targets, assays, runs, compares.
