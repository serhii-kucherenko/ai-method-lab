## Buyer
Ride-hail and mobility marketplace operations leads who must decide when to hold a match to improve passenger and driver experience instead of locking the first feasible pair.

## Outcome
A studio to model hold decisions, experience scores, match timelines, compare experience-aware hold vs first-feasible baselines, plus pricing tiers, step-by-step demo, and onboarding checklist.

## Why this paper
EXHOLD claims a two-stage experience-aware hold control system for large-scale ride-hailing matching. Clear ops/money software claim — preferred under biz-rubric-v1. Distinct from Video Track (eval) and Attest Proof (trust). Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not a DiDi rebrand.
