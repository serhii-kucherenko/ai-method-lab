# Design — Drag Wall Studio

## Brand
**Drag Wall Studio** — industrial fluid-control soft-sim. Never claim live plant control.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Cut wall drag with a closed loop you can compare.
- **Support:** Channel packs with wall-actuation plans — compare evolution-strategy closed-loop control against open-loop baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open channels → `/channels`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live plant; not certified CFD; not authors’ system
- Sources: arXiv 2607.12626 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** deep navy channel ink + cyan shear accent + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--dw-ink: #0f1c2e`, `--dw-cyan: #2a9bb5`, `--dw-mist: #e6eef3`, `--dw-line: #b7c7d1`, `--dw-warn: #c46b2f`
- **Typography:** Sora (display) + IBM Plex Sans (body)
- **Atmosphere:** soft flow mist + faint shear-line pattern, full-bleed hero
