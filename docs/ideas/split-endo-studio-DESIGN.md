# Design — Split Endo Studio

## Brand
**Split Endo Studio** — soft-sim for one-hole split endoscopy vs open laminectomy case planning. Never claim live OR control, device clearance, or clinical advice.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Split the approach. Compare the stay.
- **Support:** Case packs for thoracic OLF — compare one-hole split endoscopy against open laminectomy before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live OR; not device clearance; not clinical advice; not authors’ study brand
- Sources: Research Square 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10158488/v1 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** surgical ink + endoscope teal + cool linen (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--se-ink: #12161a`, `--se-teal: #1c5f68`, `--se-linen: #eef2f3`, `--se-line: #c4cacd`, `--se-amber: #a87a32`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft linen-field mist
