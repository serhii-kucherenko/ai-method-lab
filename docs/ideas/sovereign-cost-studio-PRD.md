# PRD — Sovereign Cost Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where sustainability leads compare sovereign AI infrastructure water–energy–emissions accounting to naive cloud-footprint baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Cost pack CRUD, models, scenarios, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Certified carbon audits, live grid metering, national policy authority, paper brand.
