# Design — Shigella Growth Studio

## Brand
**Shigella Growth Studio** — soft-sim for antibiotic-treated Shigella vs untreated diarrhea growth impact. Never claim live clinical prescribing, diagnostic clearance, or national treatment guideline authority.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Treat the episode. Protect the growth.
- **Support:** Cohort packs for child diarrhea growth impact — compare antibiotic-treated Shigella pathways against untreated diarrhea baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live prescribing; not diagnostic clearance; not national guidelines; not authors’ study brand
- Sources: medRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.10.26357688 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** epi ink + growth teal + soft chalk (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--sg-ink: #14181c`, `--sg-teal: #1a6462`, `--sg-chalk: #eef2f1`, `--sg-line: #c5cbc9`, `--sg-amber: #a87a2e`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft chalk-field mist
