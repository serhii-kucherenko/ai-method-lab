# Design — Responsible Index Studio

## Brand
**Responsible Index Studio** — soft-sim for structured country responsible-AI indexes vs naive commitment checklists. Never claim live national policy authority, certified AI audits, or government command systems.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Score the commitment. Compare the country.
- **Support:** Country packs for responsible AI governance — compare structured multi-dimension indexes against naive commitment checklists before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not national policy authority; not certified AI audit; not government command; not GIRAI brand
- Sources: arXiv 2607.14782 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** policy ink + governance teal + cool ash (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ri-ink: #13171b`, `--ri-teal: #1a5f6a`, `--ri-ash: #eef1f2`, `--ri-line: #c5cacd`, `--ri-amber: #a87830`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft ash-field mist
