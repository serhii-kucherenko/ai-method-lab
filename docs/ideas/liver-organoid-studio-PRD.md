# PRD — Liver Organoid Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where organoid-platform leads compare multicellular liver organoid (HLO) models to single-lineage hepatocyte-like cell (HLC) baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Model pack CRUD, lineages, assays, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Wet-lab validated organoid GMP claims, live patient transplant, clinical MASLD diagnosis, paper brand.
