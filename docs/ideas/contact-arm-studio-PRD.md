# PRD — Contact Arm Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where robotics leads compare contact-centric tactile+vision plans to vision-only baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Manipulator CRUD, contact plans, tactile/vision cues, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Live robot control, safety cert, TACTIC brand.
