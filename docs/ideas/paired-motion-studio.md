A studio to manage capture packs, wearer/observer configs, motion runs, and dual compares — then lock when distributed ego+exo fusion beats ego-only baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
EgoExoMoCap jointly leverages egocentric self-motion cues and exocentric views from multiple HMDs. Clear software/sim claim — unused after Mhc Design. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an EgoExoMoCap rebrand.
