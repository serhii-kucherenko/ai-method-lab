# Design — Dengue Suit Studio

## Brand
**Dengue Suit Studio** — soft-sim for CMIP6 thermal-suitability dengue risk mapping. Never claim live outbreak prediction, clinical diagnosis, or operational mosquito control deployment.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Map the heat. Count the risk.
- **Support:** Risk packs for dengue surveillance — compare CMIP6 thermal suitability against static historical baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live outbreak prediction; not clinical diagnosis; not mosquito control ops; not authors’ atlas
- Sources: medRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.02.26357126 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** map ink + mosquito teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ds-ink: #12171c`, `--ds-teal: #1f6a6e`, `--ds-mist: #eef3f4`, `--ds-line: #c3c9ce`, `--ds-amber: #b4833a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft thermal-map mist
