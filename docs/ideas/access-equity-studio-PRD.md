# PRD — Access Equity Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where autism digital-screening leads compare equity-access AI designs to accuracy-only classification baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Pathway pack CRUD, cohorts, screens, equity gates, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Clinical diagnostic claims, live EHR write-back, FDA clearance, autism diagnosis product clearance.
