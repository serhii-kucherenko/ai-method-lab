# Video Track Studio

**State:** ready_to_build  
**Slug:** `video-track-studio`  
**Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11078v1  
**Authors’ code:** none  

## Buyer
Multimodal evaluation and video large-language-model product leads who need to know whether a model actually tracks a named character across long footage — not only whether it scores well on short-clip quizzes.

## Outcome
A studio to register long-form clips, character track probes, failure diagnoses, compare track-aware scoring vs benchmark-fluency baselines, plus pricing tiers, step-by-step demo, and onboarding checklist.

## Why this paper
The paper shows open video chat models can look strong on standard benches while failing identity-sensitive outfit/character tracking in long episodes. Clear eval software claim — preferred under biz-rubric-v1. Distinct from Consult Bench (medical consults) and Attest Proof (tool proofs). Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not a rebrand.

## Business score (pre-climb)
Tier B candidate: eval buyer + bench seats; moat is character-track diagnosis vs generic VQA scores.
