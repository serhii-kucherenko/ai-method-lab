## Buyer
Antiviral antibody / mucosal-delivery analytics leads who need to compare mucus-anchoring protein (MAP) antibody conjugates against conventional monoclonal antibodies before locking a conjugate pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage conjugate packs, anchor configs, assay runs, and dual compares — then lock when mAb-MAP conjugates beat conventional mAbs while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
Conjugating antiviral antibodies with a mucus-binding protein (Trefoil Factor 3 / MAP) aims to improve mucosal retention and antiviral efficacy versus ordinary mAbs. Clear biologics A/B software claim — unused after Dhodh Screen. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
