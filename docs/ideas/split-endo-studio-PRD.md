# PRD — Split Endo Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where spine surgical analytics leads compare one-hole split endoscopy pathways to open laminectomy baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Case pack CRUD, approaches, outcomes, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Live OR claims, device clearance, clinical advice, paper brand.
