# Vision — Hold Match Studio

**Buyer:** Ride-hail and mobility marketplace operations leads who decide when to hold a driver–order match to protect passenger and driver experience instead of locking the first feasible pair.

**Outcome:** Model hold decisions, score passenger/driver experience lanes, walk match timelines, and compare experience-aware hold quality against a first-feasible baseline — before an ops team ships a brittle threshold heuristic as “matching policy.”

**Why now:** arXiv 2607.09090 (EXHOLD) shows production hold control can improve trip completion and reduce cancellations when experience tiers and guardrail-constrained hold times replace hand-tuned model thresholds. Ops budgets need a studio for that decision pattern, not another generic dispatch dashboard.

**Honesty:** Method-lab soft-sim inspired by the paper’s two-stage hold-control pattern. Not EXHOLD, not DiDi, not live marketplace production control. Authors’ code: none published with the paper abstract link we track.
