# Consult Bench Studio

**State:** ready_to_build  
**Slug:** `consult-bench-studio`  
**Paper:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09142v1  
**Authors’ code:** none  

## Buyer
Teams evaluating LLMs for online medical consultation who need multimodal (text+image) next-response benchmarks grounded in real consult moments — not generic chat evals.

## Outcome
A studio to manage consult cases, multimodal turns, department coverage, next-response scoring, and compare multimodal-aware vs text-only baselines.

## Why this paper
Clear evaluation-product software claim (MedRealMM real-world multimodal medical consults). Distinct from MSK/Cardiac/Thorax clinical apps. No public authors’ code — Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not a rebrand of MedRealMM.
