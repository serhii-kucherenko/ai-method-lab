# Design — Enhanced Flu Studio

## Brand
**Enhanced Flu Studio** — soft-sim for expanded enhanced influenza vaccine programs for adults 65+. Never claim live immunization logistics, clinical prescribing, or national policy adoption.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Expand the program. Count the winters.
- **Support:** Program packs for Nordic-style EIV planning — compare expanded enhanced flu programs against current national policy baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live logistics; not clinical prescribing; not national policy adoption; not authors’ model
- Sources: Vaccine 10.1016/j.vaccine.2026.128934 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** frost ink + Nordic teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ef-ink: #12171c`, `--ef-teal: #2a5f6a`, `--ef-mist: #eef2f4`, `--ef-line: #c4c9ce`, `--ef-amber: #b4833a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft winter-mist
