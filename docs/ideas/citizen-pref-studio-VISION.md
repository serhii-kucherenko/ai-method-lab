# Vision — Citizen Pref Studio

## Problem
AI product and policy teams often optimize for innovation speed or vendor self-regulation while public preference evidence points the other way. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares safety-first public-oversight option packs to innovation-first baselines before locking a policy pack.

## Buyer
AI policy / product governance eng leads.

## Promise
Version policy packs, configure conjoint options and country cohorts, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a surveil-gate or access-equity desk. Domain nouns: packs, options, countries, surveys, prefs, runs, compares.
