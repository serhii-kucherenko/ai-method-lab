## Buyer
Water-remediation / materials analytics leads who need to compare anionic MOF heavy-metal capture pathways against conventional sorbent baselines before locking a water pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage water packs, sorbent configs, assay runs, and dual compares — then lock when anionic MOF capture beats conventional sorbents while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
SU-102, a zirconium-based anionic MOF, rapidly captures lead, mercury, and cadmium via electrostatically driven ion exchange with reusable kinetics. Clear materials A/B software claim — unused after Blood Loss. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
