# Vision — Paired Motion Studio

## Problem
Ego-only HMD body tracking fails under occlusion and out-of-view limbs; exo-only monocular capture drifts in world space. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares distributed ego+exo fusion to ego-only baselines before locking a capture pack.

## Buyer
Embodied AI / VR-AR / mocap analytics eng leads.

## Promise
Version capture packs, configure wearers and observers, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a contact-arm or video-track desk. Domain nouns: sessions, wearers, observers, captures, runs, compares.
