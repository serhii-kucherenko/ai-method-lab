# PRD — Dengue Suit Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where public-health climate leads compare CMIP6 thermal-suitability dengue risk maps to static historical baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Risk pack CRUD, scenarios, species, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Live outbreak prediction claims, clinical diagnosis, operational mosquito control deployment, paper brand.
