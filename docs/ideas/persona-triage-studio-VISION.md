# Vision — Persona Triage Studio

## Problem
Virtual-triage and symptom-assessment products are often evaluated on cooperative, articulate simulated patients. Real users bring emotion, strategy, and communication style that can change urgency outcomes — and teams lack a bench that makes that gap visible before lock.

## Buyer
Clinical AI / virtual-care product leads and eval owners who already buy benches and red-team packs.

## Promise
Persona Triage Studio lets them version persona packs, attach conversation cases and style axes, score style-aware urgency against an idealized-patient baseline, and see disparity shifts on a scoreboard — as a method-lab soft-sim, not a clinical device.

## Differentiation
Not a consult transcript desk. Not a jobs/lifecycle shell. Domain nouns are personae, style axes, urgency runs, and disparity compares.
