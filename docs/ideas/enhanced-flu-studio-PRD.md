# PRD — Enhanced Flu Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where vaccine-program leads compare expanded enhanced influenza vaccine programs for adults 65+ to current national policy baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Program pack CRUD, countries, outcomes, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Live immunization logistics claims, clinical prescribing, national policy adoption, paper brand.
