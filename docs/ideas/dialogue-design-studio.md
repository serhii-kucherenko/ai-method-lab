## Buyer
Civic-tech / platform trust-and-safety / deliberative media leads who need to compare productive open-minded dialogue designs against engagement-maximizing baselines before locking a feed pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage feed packs, open-minded badges, topic runs, and dual compares — then lock when productive-dialogue designs beat engagement-maximizing baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
An RCT with generative AI-simulated discussions finds that open-minded badge designs shift language and emotion toward more constructive dialogue — without claiming attitude change. Clear HCI/software claim — unused after Citizen Pref. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
