# Design — Disaster Liaison Studio

## Brand
**Disaster Liaison Studio** — soft-sim for pediatric-perinatal disaster liaison vs generic HQ coordination. Never claim live emergency dispatch, clinical triage authority, or government command systems.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Liaison before the surge.
- **Support:** Response packs for pediatric and perinatal disaster coordination — compare embedded liaison frameworks against generic headquarters baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live dispatch; not clinical triage authority; not government command; not authors’ DLPPM brand
- Sources: Pediatrics International 10.1111/ped.70488 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** ops ink + signal teal + cool ash (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--dl-ink: #13171b`, `--dl-teal: #1a5f6a`, `--dl-ash: #eef1f2`, `--dl-line: #c5cacd`, `--dl-amber: #a87830`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft ash-field mist
