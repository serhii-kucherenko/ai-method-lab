# PRD — Shigella Growth Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where child-health epidemiology leads compare antibiotic-treated Shigella pathways to untreated diarrhea growth baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Cohort pack CRUD, episodes, growth assays, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Live clinical prescribing, diagnostic clearance, national guideline authority, paper brand.
