# PRD — Citizen Pref Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where AI policy leads compare safety-first public-oversight regulatory option packs to innovation-first self-regulation baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Policy pack CRUD, options, countries, surveys, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Live regulatory authority, government deployment claims, certified polling product, paper brand.
