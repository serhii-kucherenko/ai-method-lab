## Buyer

A studio to manage case packs, approach configs, outcome runs, and dual compares — then lock when OSE pathways beat open laminectomy baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
A multicenter propensity study compares one-hole split endoscopy versus open laminectomy for single-level thoracic ossification of the ligamentum flavum. Clear surgical A/B software claim — unused after Immunize Impact. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
