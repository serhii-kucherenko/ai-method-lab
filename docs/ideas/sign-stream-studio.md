## Buyer
Accessibility and language-access product leads who need sentence-level sign language translation that can run as a real-time stream — not only offline batch gloss dumps.

## Outcome
A studio to manage sign streams, sentence segments, latency budgets, compare real-time stream quality vs offline-batch baselines, plus pricing tiers, step-by-step demo, and onboarding checklist.

## Why this paper
Clear software claim toward real-time sentence-level sign language translation. A11y / institutional buyer story — preferred under biz-rubric-v1 over clinical carepath clones. Distinct from Tactile Chart (charts) and Hold Match (ride-hail). Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not a rebrand of the paper stack.

## Business score (pre-climb)
Tier B candidate: a11y buyer + institution seats / stream minutes; moat is real-time sentence stream vs offline batch.
