# Design — Map Anchor Studio

## Brand
**Map Anchor Studio** — soft-sim for mucus-anchoring MAP antibody conjugates vs conventional mAbs. Never claim wet-lab validation, clinical antiviral advice, IND/NDA readiness, or live patient dosing.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Anchor the antibody. Compare the conjugate.
- **Support:** Conjugate packs for mucosal antiviral planning — compare MAP-anchored antibodies against conventional mAbs before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not wet-lab validated; not clinical antiviral advice; not IND/NDA ready; not patient dosing; not authors’ brand
- Sources: ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15003962/v2 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** mucus ink + mucosal teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ma-ink: #14181c`, `--ma-teal: #1a5c66`, `--ma-mist: #eef3f4`, `--ma-line: #c5cbce`, `--ma-amber: #a67a30`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft mist-field atmosphere
