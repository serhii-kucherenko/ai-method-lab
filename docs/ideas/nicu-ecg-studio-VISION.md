# Vision — Nicu Ecg Studio

## Problem
PPG-to-ECG reconstruction often relies on artificial signal alignment that fails for neonates. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares alignment-free dual-stream transformers to alignment-dependent baselines before locking an ecg pack.

## Buyer
NICU analytics / neonatal monitoring eng leads.

## Promise
Version ecg packs, configure PPG channels and inpaint recipes, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a track-map or accel-pd desk. Domain nouns: ecgs, ppg, inpaints, runs, compares.
