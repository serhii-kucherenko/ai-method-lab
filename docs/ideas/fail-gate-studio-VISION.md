# Vision — Fail Gate Studio

## Who
Health LLM evaluation and safety QA leads who must decide whether a model is safe to release — not clinicians charting carepaths.

## Outcome
A soft-sim **eval / safety release-gate bench** where teams register fail cases, maintain severity and safety-gate taxonomies, inspect boundary reasons, and compare **fail-gate taxonomy diagnosis (A)** against a **correctness-only / naive accuracy baseline (B)**.

## Why now
MedFailBench-style work shows medical AI failures are often *boundary and taxonomy* problems, not mere accuracy misses. Correctness-only benches greenlight unsafe answers. Buyers need a release gate that explains *why* a case failed a safety boundary.

## Money honesty
Method-lab packaging: **bench seats + private fail-case packs**. Not a live hospital deployment, not clinical certification, not MedFailBench rebrand.

## Distinct from portfolio
- Not Consult Bench (consult-turn multimodal scoring)
- Not Agent Safety (CFG/monitor fleets)
- Not a jobs/lifecycle/scenario desk shell
- Domain objects: fail cases, gate taxonomies, boundary inspections, dual compare, scoreboard, case packs
