# PRD — Margin Fusion Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where surgical imaging leads compare marker-free deformable margin fusion to marker-based baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Case pack CRUD, specimen scans, fusion configs, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Surgical device clearance, live OR, paper brand.
