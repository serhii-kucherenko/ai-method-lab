# Design — Mof Capture Studio

## Brand
**Mof Capture Studio** — soft-sim for anionic MOF heavy-metal capture vs conventional sorbents. Never claim live plant control, certified water audits, or municipal procurement authority.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Capture the metal. Compare the sorbent.
- **Support:** Water packs for heavy-metal remediation — compare anionic MOF capture against conventional sorbents before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live plant control; not certified water audit; not municipal procurement; not SU-102 brand
- Sources: ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15006194/v1 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** water ink + zircon teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--mc-ink: #12161a`, `--mc-teal: #1a5f68`, `--mc-mist: #eef2f3`, `--mc-line: #c4cacd`, `--mc-amber: #a87a32`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft mist-field atmosphere
