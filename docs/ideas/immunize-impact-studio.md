## Buyer
Public-health / immunization-program analytics leads who need to compare immunization-linked under-five mortality panels against coverage-only dashboards before locking an impact pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage impact packs, country panels, antigen coverage runs, and dual compares — then lock when immunization-linked mortality models beat coverage-only baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
An ecological panel study links routine childhood immunization coverage (especially DTP3) to under-five mortality across Sub-Saharan African countries, 2000–2024. Clear health-program software claim — unused after Enhanced Flu. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
