## Buyer
Materials / soft-matter electrolyte analytics leads who need to compare dynamic charge-regulating hydrogel ion transport against fixed-charge baselines before locking a gel pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage gel packs, charge configs, salt runs, and dual compares — then lock when dynamic charge regulation beats fixed-charge baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
Large-scale molecular simulations show dynamic charge regulation can decouple ion mobility from binding strength in weak polyelectrolyte hydrogels. Clear materials soft-sim software claim — unused after Fluoride Label. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
