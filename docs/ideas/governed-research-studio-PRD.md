# PRD — Governed Research Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where institutional research leads compare governed end-to-end research workflows to ungated agent baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Study pack CRUD, governance gates, workflow configs, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
IRB clearance, live PHI, paper brand.
