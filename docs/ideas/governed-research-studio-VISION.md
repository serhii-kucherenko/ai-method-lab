# Vision — Governed Research Studio

## Problem
Ungated research agents risk privacy and process drift. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares governed end-to-end workflows to ungated agent baselines before locking a study pack.

## Buyer
Institutional research / bioinformatics eng leads.

## Promise
Version study packs, configure gates and workflows, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not an enterprise-agent ERP desk. Domain nouns: studies, gates, workflows, runs, compares.
