# Design — Dialogue Design Studio

## Brand
**Dialogue Design Studio** — soft-sim for productive open-minded social dialogue feed designs. Never claim live social network deployment, content moderation authority, or attitude-change clearance.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Design the feed that rewards open minds, not outrage.
- **Support:** Feed packs for civic dialogue — compare productive open-minded designs against engagement-maximizing baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live social network; not moderation authority; not attitude-change clearance; not authors’ platform brand
- Sources: SocArXiv dngcj · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** dialogue ink + open teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--dd-ink: #14191f`, `--dd-teal: #2a6a68`, `--dd-mist: #eef3f2`, `--dd-line: #c2c9c7`, `--dd-amber: #b8873a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft dialogue-feed mist
