## Buyer
Health LLM evaluation and safety QA leads who need to inspect *why* medical AI answers fail safety gates — not only whether they look correct.

## Outcome
A studio to manage fail cases, severity and gate taxonomies, boundary inspections, compare fail-gate diagnosis vs correctness-only baselines, plus pricing, demo, onboarding, ≥5 named flows, and eval-platform must-haves.

## Why this paper
MedFailBench claims a clinician-built benchmark focused on medical AI safety boundary failures and error taxonomies. Clear eval software claim — preferred under biz-rubric-v1 over clinical carepath clones. Distinct from Consult Bench (next-reply multimodal scoring). Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not a MedFailBench rebrand.

## Business score (pre-climb)
Tier B candidate: eval/safety buyer + bench seats; moat is fail-gate taxonomy vs accuracy-only benches.
