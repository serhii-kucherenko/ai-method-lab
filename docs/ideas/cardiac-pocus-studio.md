## Buyer
Pulmonary / point-of-care ultrasound analytics leads who need to compare cardiac POCUS COPD detection patterns against lung-ultrasound baselines before locking an exam pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage exam packs, pattern configs, assay runs, and dual compares — then lock when cardiac POCUS patterns beat lung-ultrasound baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
A multicenter prospective study finds specific cardiac point-of-care ultrasound patterns—not lung ultrasound—independently associate with COPD detection among adults referred for pulmonary function testing. Clear diagnostic A/B software claim — unused after Shigella Growth. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
