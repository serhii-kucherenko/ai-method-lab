# Vision — Video Track Studio

**Buyer:** Multimodal evaluation and Video-LLM product leads who ship long-form video QA and need to know whether scores reflect named-character tracking — not short-clip fluency.

**Outcome:** Register long-form clips and cast characters, run track probes (name-swap / identity sensitivity), diagnose failure modes, and compare track-aware diagnosis quality against a benchmark-fluency baseline — before a high InfiniBench-style number ships as “the model watches.”

**Why now:** arXiv 2607.11078 shows open Video-LLMs can look strong on global appearance benches while largely ignoring who the question names. Eval budgets need a diagnostic studio for character-track claims, not another generic VQA scoreboard.

**Honesty:** Method-lab soft-sim inspired by the paper’s diagnostic pattern. Not a rebrand of the authors’ toolkit. Not a claim that production models “watch” video. Authors’ code: none published with the paper abstract link we track.
