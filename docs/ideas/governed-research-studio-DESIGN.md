# Design — Governed Research Studio

## Brand
**Governed Research Studio** — soft-sim for governed end-to-end research workflows. Never claim IRB clearance or live PHI write-back.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Research that stays inside the gate.
- **Support:** Study packs with governance gates — compare against ungated agent baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open studies → `/studies`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not IRB cleared; not live PHI; not authors’ system
- Sources: arXiv 2607.11084 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** institutional navy + soft brass + cool parchment (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--gr-ink: #142033`, `--gr-brass: #b08d4a`, `--gr-parchment: #eef1f4`, `--gr-line: #c2c8d0`, `--gr-teal: #3a6f75`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Karla (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft parchment mist + faint study-grid
