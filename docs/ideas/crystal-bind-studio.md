## Buyer
Materials characterization and coatings / crystal informatics leads who already juggle structure, diffraction, electronic density, and written specs — and buy design / search tooling when modalities disagree.

## Outcome
A studio to register crystal packs, align multimodal descriptors into a shared bind space, compare multimodal retrieval vs single-modality baselines, and keep honesty fences on soft-sim embeddings.

## Why this paper
MatBind claims a shared embedding space across atomic layout, XRD-like patterns, density of states, and natural language. Industrial software wedge similar to Optical Stack — preferred under biz-rubric-v1 over more clinical carepath clones. No public authors’ code in the digest — Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not a rebrand of MatBind.
