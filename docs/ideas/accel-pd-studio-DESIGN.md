# Design — Accel PD Studio

## Brand
**Accel PD Studio** — soft-sim for multi-channel wearable physical-activity representations aimed at Parkinson risk signals. Never claim clinical diagnostic use, live device write-back, or FDA clearance. Not PABformer.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Read the free-living activity signal, not just the clinic snapshot.
- **Support:** Accel packs for wearable physical-activity representation — compare multi-channel transformers against handcrafted PA-feature baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open accels → `/accels`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not diagnostic; not live device write-back; not FDA cleared; not authors’ system; not PABformer
- Sources: medRxiv 10.1101/2025.08.12.25333460 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** motion ink + accel teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ap-ink: #171c24`, `--ap-teal: #2a6f6a`, `--ap-mist: #eef2f4`, `--ap-line: #c3c9ce`, `--ap-amber: #c4923a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft multi-channel waveform mist
