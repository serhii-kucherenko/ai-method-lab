# Vision — Crystal Bind Studio

**Display:** Crystal Bind Studio  
**Slug:** `crystal-bind-studio`  
**Paper input:** [MatBind (arXiv:2607.08470)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08470v1) — shared embedding space across structure, diffraction-like patterns, density of states, and language.  
**Authors’ code:** none published.

## Buyer

Materials characterization and coatings / crystal informatics leads who already juggle CIF-like structure notes, powder-diffraction fingerprints, electronic DOS summaries, and written specs — and buy search tooling when those modalities disagree.

## Outcome

A studio to register **crystal packs**, keep **four descriptor lanes** (structure / diffraction / DOS / language), project them into a **shared bind space**, and **retrieve / compare** multimodal bind quality against a single-modality baseline — with an honesty fence that this is soft-sim embeddings for a method-lab product, not MatBind and not measured wet-lab spectra.

## Why now

Industrial software wedge (similar buyer gravity to Optical Stack): desktop/seats money hook; moat is multimodal bind vs single-modality search. Business scorecard: **tier B** (~68–72). Prefer this over more clinical carepath clones.

## Non-goals

- Rebranding as MatBind  
- Desk IA (`/jobs` `/lifecycle` `/scenario` `/batch` `/audit` `/goldens`)  
- Claiming wet-lab measured spectra without honesty  
- Cloning Edge Quant packs/channels or Optical Stack briefs/stacks IA
