# PRD — Ageing Wisely Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where geriatric digital mental-health leads compare therapist-supported internet CBT designs to waitlist or self-guided baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Care pack CRUD, cohorts, modules, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Clinical diagnosis claims, live therapist replacement, regulated digital therapeutic clearance, paper brand.
