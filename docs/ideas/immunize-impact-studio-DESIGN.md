# Design — Immunize Impact Studio

## Brand
**Immunize Impact Studio** — soft-sim for immunization-linked under-five mortality panels. Never claim live immunization logistics, clinical prescribing, or national policy authority.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Coverage that counts for survival.
- **Support:** Impact packs for childhood immunization — compare immunization-linked mortality panels against coverage-only dashboards before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live logistics; not clinical prescribing; not national policy authority; not authors’ study brand
- Sources: Research Square 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10174350/v1 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** clinic ink + vaccine teal + soft chalk (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ii-ink: #14181d`, `--ii-teal: #1a6b63`, `--ii-chalk: #eef2f1`, `--ii-line: #c5cbc9`, `--ii-amber: #b07a2e`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft chalk-field mist
