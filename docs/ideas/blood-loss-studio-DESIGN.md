# Design — Blood Loss Studio

## Brand
**Blood Loss Studio** — soft-sim for weighed-swab measured vs haemoglobin-calculated blood loss after caesarean birth. Never claim live clinical advice, EMR write-back, or device clearance.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Weigh the loss. Compare the calc.
- **Support:** Birth packs for caesarean blood-loss methods — compare weighed-swab measurement against haemoglobin-calculated baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not clinical advice; not EMR write-back; not device clearance; not authors’ study brand
- Sources: medRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.16.26358295 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** clinic ink + measure teal + soft chalk (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--bl-ink: #14181c`, `--bl-teal: #1a6460`, `--bl-chalk: #eef2f1`, `--bl-line: #c5cbc9`, `--bl-amber: #a87a2e`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft chalk-field mist
