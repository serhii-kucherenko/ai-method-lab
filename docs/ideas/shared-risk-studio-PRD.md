# PRD — Shared Risk Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where biobank leads compare shared multi-disease health representations to disease-specific baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Cohort pack CRUD, modality schemas, risk queries, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
CDS clearance, live EHR, paper brand.
