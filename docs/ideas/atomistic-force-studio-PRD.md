# PRD — Atomistic Force Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where comp-chem leads compare foundation-model atomistic simulations to classical force-field baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Sim pack CRUD, force configs, trajectories, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
DFT manufacturing validation, live HPC write-back, paper brand.
