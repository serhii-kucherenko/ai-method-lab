## Buyer
Obstetric / perinatal analytics leads who need to compare weighed-swab measured blood loss against haemoglobin-calculated baselines after caesarean birth before locking a birth pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage birth packs, method configs, assay runs, and dual compares — then lock when weighed-swab measured loss beats haemoglobin-calculated baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
A study compares direct measured blood loss (weighing swabs and pads) versus calculated loss from maternal weight and haemoglobin change after caesarean birth. Clear obstetric A/B software claim — unused after Responsible Index. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
