# Vision — Latent Path Studio

## Problem
Adolescent symptom studies often model anxiety, conduct, or psychotic-like experiences in isolation and miss joint high-risk paths. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares multi-dimensional latent trajectory classes to single-domain baselines before locking a cohort pack.

## Buyer
Adolescent mental-health analytics / digital screening product eng leads.

## Promise
Version cohort packs, configure predictors and trajectory recipes, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not an async-neuro or therapy-prompt desk. Domain nouns: packs, cohorts, predictors, trajectories, outcomes, runs, compares.
