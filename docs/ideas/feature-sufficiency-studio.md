**State:** framed

## Buyer
Healthcare AI and clinical model-ops leads who need to know whether the variables available for a patient are enough to reach full-feature model performance — before they trust a partial-observation prediction.

## Outcome
A studio to manage feature packs, observation masks, sufficiency checks, and full-feature vs partial-feature compares — then lock an eval pack when sufficiency deltas are understood.

## Why this paper
Feature Sufficiency Analysis (FSA) claims a computational method that checks whether currently available clinical variables suffice for full-feature-capable performance. Clear software/eval claim — unused after Persona Triage. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an FSA rebrand.

## Business score (pre-climb)
Tier B candidate: clinical ML ops buyer + feature/eval packs; moat is sufficiency-under-partial-observation vs accuracy-only benches.
