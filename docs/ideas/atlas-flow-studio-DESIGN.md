# Design — Atlas Flow Studio

## Brand
**Atlas Flow Studio** — soft-sim for integrated brain-atlas registration and quantification. Never claim live microscope control, clinical diagnostic use, or FDA clearance. Not NeuroFlow.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** One atlas flow instead of five packages.
- **Support:** Atlas packs for registration and region quantification — compare an integrated workflow against fragmented multi-tool baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open atlases → `/atlases`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not diagnostic; not live microscope control; not FDA cleared; not authors’ system; not NeuroFlow
- Sources: bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.15.737186 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** atlas ink + register teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--af-ink: #1a1f28`, `--af-teal: #2a7a7a`, `--af-mist: #eef2f5`, `--af-line: #c2c8ce`, `--af-amber: #b8893a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft section-grid mist
