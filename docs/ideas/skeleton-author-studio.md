## Buyer
Accessibility / data-viz leads who need to compare visual authoring of non-visual data experiences against naive linear baselines before locking an experience pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage experience packs, navigation skeletons, label templates, and dual compares — then lock when scaffolded authoring beats naive linear baselines while keeping accessibility honesty.

## Why this paper
The paper proposes Skeleton, a visual authoring tool for non-visual data experiences with dual-view editing and scaffolding. Clear software/eval claim — unused after Assay Guard. Distinct from tactile-chart. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
