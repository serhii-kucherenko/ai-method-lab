# Design — Sovereign Cost Studio

## Brand
**Sovereign Cost Studio** — soft-sim for sovereign AI infrastructure environmental costs. Never claim certified carbon audits, live grid metering, or national policy authority.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Sovereign AI, priced in water and watts.
- **Support:** Cost packs with water–energy–emissions accounting — compare against naive cloud-footprint baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open costs → `/costs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not certified audit; not live grid metering; not policy authority; not authors’ system
- Sources: arXiv 2607.13443 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** earth ink + watt teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--sc-ink: #1a2220`, `--sc-teal: #2f7a6e`, `--sc-mist: #eef2f1`, `--sc-line: #c4cbc8`, `--sc-amber: #c4923a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Source Sans 3 (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft earth mist + faint infra-grid
