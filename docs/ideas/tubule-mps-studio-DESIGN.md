# Design — Tubule Mps Studio

## Brand
**Tubule Mps Studio** — soft-sim for voclosporin mitochondrial preservation vs cyclosporine A in proximal-tubule MPS. Never claim wet-lab MPS validation, transplant dosing advice, IND/NDA readiness, or live patient care.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Preserve the tubule. Compare the regimen.
- **Support:** Tubule packs for calcineurin soft-sims — compare voclosporin MPS preservation against cyclosporine A before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not wet-lab validated; not transplant dosing advice; not IND/NDA ready; not live patient care; not authors’ brand
- Sources: bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.07.737071 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** nephron ink + filtrate teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--tm-ink: #14181c`, `--tm-teal: #1a5c66`, `--tm-mist: #eef3f4`, `--tm-line: #c5cbce`, `--tm-amber: #a67a30`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft mist-field atmosphere
