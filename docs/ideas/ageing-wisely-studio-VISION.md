# Vision — Ageing Wisely Studio

## Problem
Self-guided or waitlist mental-health paths under-serve older adults with anxiety or depression. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares therapist-supported iCBT designs to waitlist/self-guided baselines before locking a care pack.

## Buyer
Geriatric digital mental-health / care-delivery analytics eng leads.

## Promise
Version care packs, configure co-design and cohorts, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a coload-order or therapy-prompt desk. Domain nouns: packs, cohorts, modules, sessions, assays, runs, compares.
