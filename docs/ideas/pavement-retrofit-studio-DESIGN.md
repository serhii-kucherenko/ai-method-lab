# Design — Pavement Retrofit Studio

## Brand
**Pavement Retrofit Studio** — soft-sim for photocatalytic pavement retrofit vs conventional preservation. Never claim live road construction control, certified emissions audits, or municipal procurement authority.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Retrofit the surface. Compare the air.
- **Support:** Corridor packs for photocatalytic pavement — compare retrofit pathways against conventional preservation before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live construction; not certified emissions audit; not municipal procurement; not PlusTi brand
- Sources: ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15006236/v1 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** asphalt ink + photocatalytic teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--pr-ink: #121518`, `--pr-teal: #1b6360`, `--pr-mist: #eef2f1`, `--pr-line: #c4cac8`, `--pr-amber: #a87a2e`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft mist-field atmosphere
