# Design — Usher Dual Studio

## Brand
**Usher Dual Studio** — soft-sim for MYO7A gene supplementation vs Myo7b activation in Usher 1B pathway packs. Never claim wet-lab validation, IND/NDA readiness, patient dosing, or clinical gene-therapy advice.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Two pathways. One compare.
- **Support:** Allele packs for Usher 1B planning — compare MYO7A supplementation against Myo7b activation before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not wet-lab validated; not IND/NDA ready; not patient dosing; not clinical gene-therapy advice; not authors’ brand
- Sources: bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.02.736025 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** sensory ink + cochlear teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ud-ink: #14181c`, `--ud-teal: #1a5c66`, `--ud-mist: #eef3f4`, `--ud-line: #c5cbce`, `--ud-amber: #a67a30`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft mist-field atmosphere
