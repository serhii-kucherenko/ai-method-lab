# Vision — Terrain Fire Studio

## One-liner
Wildfire GIS leads refresh aging 3D terrain with new aerial photos using physics-aware alignment — then prove it beats naive photo-on-DEM overlays before crews trust the map.

## Buyer
Agency and land-management GIS / fire-planning leads who own terrain packs for wildfire-prone landscapes. They pay for seats + refresh compute when outdated ground costs evacuation and resource decisions.

## Outcome
A studio to register versioned terrain packs, ingest aerial refreshes, run alignment plans, compare physics-aware refresh quality against a naive overlay baseline, audit/export, and onboard the org — with soft-sim honesty that this is not live dispatch or survey certification.

## Why now
LTM-style large-scale terrain modeling for wildfire landscapes (arXiv 2607.08711) motivates the pattern: align new aerials to older 3D maps. Method Lab ships an industrial GIS product inspired by that claim — not an LTM rebrand, not a desk clone.
