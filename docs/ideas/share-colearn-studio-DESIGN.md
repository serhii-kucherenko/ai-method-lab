# Design — Share Colearn Studio

## Brand
**Share Colearn Studio** — soft-sim for human–AI co-learning of disease activity labels. Never claim clinical diagnostic use, live EHR write-back, or FDA clearance.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Labels that learn with the clinician.
- **Support:** Colearn packs for disease activity labeling — compare human–AI co-learning against AI-only baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open colearns → `/colearns`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not diagnostic; not live EHR write-back; not FDA cleared; not authors’ system
- Sources: medRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.16.26358271 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** chart ink + share teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--sc-ink: #171c24`, `--sc-teal: #2f7a7a`, `--sc-mist: #eef2f4`, `--sc-line: #c3c9ce`, `--sc-amber: #c4923a`
- **Typography:** Literata (display) + Figtree (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft chart mist + faint label-grid
