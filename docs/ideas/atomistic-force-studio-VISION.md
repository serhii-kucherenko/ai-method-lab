# Vision — Atomistic Force Studio

## Problem
Classical force fields miss reactive accuracy; teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares foundation-model atomistics to classical baselines before locking a sim pack.

## Buyer
Computational chemistry / drug-design simulation eng leads.

## Promise
Version sim packs, configure forces and trajectories, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a kinetics or molecule-sample desk. Domain nouns: sims, forces, trajectories, runs, compares.
