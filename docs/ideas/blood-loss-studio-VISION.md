# Vision — Blood Loss Studio

## Problem
Haemoglobin-calculated blood-loss baselines hide whether weighed-swab measurement is more trustworthy after caesarean birth. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares measured vs calculated methods before locking a birth pack.

## Buyer
Obstetric / perinatal analytics eng leads.

## Promise
Version birth packs, configure methods and assays, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a responsible-index or cardiac-pocus desk. Domain nouns: packs, births, methods, assays, runs, compares.
