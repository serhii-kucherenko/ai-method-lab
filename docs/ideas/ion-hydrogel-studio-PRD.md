# PRD — Ion Hydrogel Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where materials leads compare dynamic charge-regulating hydrogel ion transport to fixed-charge baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Gel pack CRUD, charges, salts, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Wet-lab validated membrane manufacturing claims, live plant ionics, commercial battery cell qualification, paper brand.
