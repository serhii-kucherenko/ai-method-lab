# Design — Surveil Gate Studio

## Brand
**Surveil Gate Studio** — soft-sim for six-pillar trust governance of generative AI in digital public health surveillance. Never claim live national surveillance deployment, clinical diagnostic use, or regulatory certification. Never brand as TRUST-GPH.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Explainability is not a governance plan.
- **Support:** Surveillance packs for generative public-health AI — compare six-pillar trust governance against explainability-only baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not live national surveillance; not clinical diagnostic; not regulatory certification; not authors’ framework brand
- Sources: Frontiers DOI 10.3389/fpubh.2026.1901894 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** civic ink + gate teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--sg-ink: #151a22`, `--sg-teal: #2a6670`, `--sg-mist: #eef2f5`, `--sg-line: #c3c9ce`, `--sg-amber: #b8863a`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft surveillance-signal mist
