# PRD — Async Neuro Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where multi-center neurology study leads compare standardized async video neuro exams to ad-hoc exam baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Exam pack CRUD, sites, protocols, videos, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Clinical diagnostic claims, live telehealth write-back, FDA clearance, paper brand.
