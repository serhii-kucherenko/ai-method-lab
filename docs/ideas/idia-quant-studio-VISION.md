# Vision — Idia Quant Studio

## Problem
Naive DIA misses low-abundance regulatory proteins in single-cell proteomics. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares informed DIA to naive DIA baselines before locking a quant pack.

## Buyer
Single-cell proteomics / mass-spec analytics eng leads.

## Promise
Version quant packs, configure spectra and targets, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not an assay-guard or mol-sample desk. Domain nouns: quants, spectra, targets, runs, compares.
