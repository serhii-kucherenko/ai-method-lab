# Design — Ion Hydrogel Studio

## Brand
**Ion Hydrogel Studio** — soft-sim for dynamic charge-regulating hydrogel ion transport. Never claim wet-lab validated membrane manufacturing, live plant ionics, or commercial battery cell qualification.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Regulate the charge. Free the ion.
- **Support:** Gel packs for soft-matter electrolytes — compare dynamic charge regulation against fixed-charge baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not membrane manufacturing; not live plant ionics; not battery cell qualification; not authors’ system
- Sources: ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15004897 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** lab ink + aqua sea + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ih-ink: #12171c`, `--ih-sea: #1e6a6d`, `--ih-mist: #eef3f4`, `--ih-line: #c3c9ce`, `--ih-copper: #a8763d`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft hydrogel-ion mist
