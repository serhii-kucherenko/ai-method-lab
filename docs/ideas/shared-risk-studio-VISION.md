# Vision — Shared Risk Studio

## Problem
Disease-specific risk models fragment biobank work. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares shared multi-disease representations to disease-specific baselines before locking a cohort pack.

## Buyer
Population health / biobank ML eng leads.

## Promise
Version cohort packs, configure modalities and risk queries, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not a federated-CVD or HCC-reason desk. Domain nouns: cohorts, modalities, queries, runs, compares.
