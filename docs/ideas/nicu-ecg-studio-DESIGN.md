# Design — Nicu Ecg Studio

## Brand
**Nicu Ecg Studio** — soft-sim for alignment-free PPG-guided neonatal ECG inpainting. Never claim clinical diagnostic use, live device write-back, or FDA clearance.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Recover the ECG segment without forcing a fake alignment.
- **Support:** Ecg packs for PPG-guided neonatal inpainting — compare alignment-free dual-stream transformers against alignment-dependent baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open ecgs → `/ecgs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not diagnostic; not live device write-back; not FDA cleared; not authors’ system
- Sources: medRxiv 10.64898/2026.07.06.26357087 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** monitor ink + pulse teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ne-ink: #171c24`, `--ne-teal: #2a6f6a`, `--ne-mist: #eef2f4`, `--ne-line: #c3c9ce`, `--ne-amber: #c4923a`
- **Typography:** Source Serif 4 (display) + IBM Plex Sans (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft dual-stream waveform mist
