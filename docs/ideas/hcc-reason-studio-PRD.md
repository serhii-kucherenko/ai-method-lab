# PRD — HCC Reason Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where hepatology leads compare clinical-reasoning LLM HCC risk stratification to non-reasoning baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Pathway pack CRUD, risk schemas, reasoner configs, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
CDS clearance, live EHR, paper brand.
