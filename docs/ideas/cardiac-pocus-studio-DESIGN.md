# Design — Cardiac Pocus Studio

## Brand
**Cardiac Pocus Studio** — soft-sim for cardiac POCUS COPD patterns vs lung-ultrasound baselines. Never claim live diagnostic clearance, clinical advice, or PACS write-back.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Look at the heart. Compare the lung.
- **Support:** Exam packs for COPD point-of-care ultrasound — compare cardiac POCUS patterns against lung-ultrasound baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not diagnostic clearance; not clinical advice; not PACS write-back; not authors’ study brand
- Sources: Research Square 10.21203/rs.3.rs-9994279/v1 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** scan ink + probe teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--cp-ink: #12161a`, `--cp-teal: #1a5f68`, `--cp-mist: #eef2f3`, `--cp-line: #c4cacd`, `--cp-amber: #a87a32`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft mist-field atmosphere
