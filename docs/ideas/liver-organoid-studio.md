## Buyer
Organoid-platform / MASLD screening analytics leads who need to compare multicellular liver organoid (HLO) models against single-lineage hepatocyte-like cell (HLC) baselines before locking a model pack.

## Outcome
A studio to manage model packs, lineage configs, assay runs, and dual compares — then lock when multicellular HLOs beat single-lineage HLC baselines while keeping soft-sim honesty.

## Why this paper
A 10-day hepatic differentiation method yields functional HLCs and multicellular HLOs that better recapitulate native liver complexity for MASLD modelling. Clear organoid-platform software claim — unused after Ion Hydrogel. Method Lab product inspired by the pattern, not an authors’ rebrand.
