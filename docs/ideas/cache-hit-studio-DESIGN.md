# Design — Cache Hit Studio

## Brand
**Cache Hit Studio** — soft-sim for structured computational hit-finding packs. Never claim wet-lab validated hits, live ELN write-back, or FDA clearance. Not CACHE.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Hit-find against a real baseline, not a lucky dock.
- **Support:** Hit packs for immuno-oncology targets — compare structured computational hit-finding against naive docking baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open hits → `/hits`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not wet-lab validated; not live ELN write-back; not FDA cleared; not authors’ system; not CACHE
- Sources: ChemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.15005792 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** assay ink + hit teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--ch-ink: #171c22`, `--ch-teal: #2a6f6a`, `--ch-mist: #eef2f4`, `--ch-line: #c3c9ce`, `--ch-amber: #c4923a`
- **Typography:** Source Serif 4 (display) + IBM Plex Sans (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft ligand-pocket mist
