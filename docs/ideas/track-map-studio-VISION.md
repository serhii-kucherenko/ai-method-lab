# Vision — Track Map Studio

## Problem
Most deformable reconstruction pipelines are offline and depend on accurate camera trajectory priors from robotic kinematics. Teams lack a soft-sim bench that compares online deformable SLAM to offline kinematics-prior baselines before locking a track pack.

## Buyer
Surgical robotics / intraoperative vision eng leads.

## Promise
Version track packs, configure poses and reconstructions, run dual compares, lock when deltas and honesty are understood — method-lab soft-sim only.

## Differentiation
Not an omic-prior or cardiac-ct desk. Domain nouns: tracks, poses, reconstructions, runs, compares.
