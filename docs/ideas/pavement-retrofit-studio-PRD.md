# PRD — Pavement Retrofit Studio

## Goal
Soft-sim studio where infrastructure climate leads compare photocatalytic pavement retrofit pathways to conventional pavement-preservation baselines.

## Must-have (≥25)
Corridor pack CRUD, treatments, assays, dual scorer A/B, compare, scoreboard, audit, export, webhook, org/members, search, auth, pagination, rate limit, goldens ≥30, try.html, pricing, demo, onboarding, flows (≥5), honesty, landing, settings.

## Non-goals
Live construction control, certified emissions audits, municipal procurement authority, paper brand.
