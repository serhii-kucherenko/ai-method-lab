# Design — Ageing Wisely Studio

## Brand
**Ageing Wisely Studio** — soft-sim for therapist-supported internet CBT for older adults. Never claim clinical diagnosis, live therapist replacement, or regulated digital therapeutic clearance.

## Landing brief
- **Headline:** Support the session. Keep the dignity.
- **Support:** Care packs for older-adult digital mental health — compare therapist-supported iCBT against waitlist or self-guided baselines before you lock a pack.
- **Primary CTA:** Open packs → `/packs`
- **Secondary:** See demo → `/demo`
- Honesty: soft-sim only; not clinical diagnosis; not therapist replacement; not regulated digital therapeutic; not authors’ program
- Sources: PsyArXiv hukx9 · authors’ code: none published

## Visual direction
- **Palette:** calm ink + sage teal + cool mist (not purple, not cream-terracotta, not broadsheet)
  - `--aw-ink: #16181c`, `--aw-sage: #3d6a5e`, `--aw-mist: #eef2f1`, `--aw-line: #c5cac7`, `--aw-amber: #a87d3c`
- **Typography:** Fraunces (display) + Manrope (body)
- Full-bleed hero with soft session mist
