# Surveil Gate Studio — what we learned

## Claim
Public-health AI governance / digital surveillance leads need a soft-sim bench that compares six-pillar trust governance to explainability-only baselines before locking a surveillance pack — not a fail-gate desk or isomorphic clone.

## Category practices shipped
- Eval bench: ≥30 dual-impl goldens (`sg-001…sg-030`), A/B compare, scoreboard
- Domain IA: packs / pillars / policies / signals / audits (no `/jobs` `/lifecycle` `/scenario`)
- Platform: bearer auth, members, audit, export JSON/CSV, webhook HMAC, rate limit, pagination
- Commercial surfaces: `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/onboarding`, `/flows` (≥5), `/honesty`
- Offline `try.html` + live `test:app-up`

## Honesty
Never claim live national surveillance deployment, clinical diagnostic use, regulatory certification, or the TRUST-GPH brand. Not the authors’ system.

## Deferred
Live national surveillance orchestration, clinical decision support, and regulatory certification pipelines — out of soft-sim scope.
