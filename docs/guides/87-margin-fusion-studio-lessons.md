# Margin Fusion Studio — lessons

## What we shipped
A soft-sim studio for surgical imaging leads: case packs, specimen scans, deformable fusion configs, dual A/B (marker-free vs marker-based), scoreboard, and platform must-haves.

## Keep
- Domain IA (`/cases` `/specimens` `/fusions`) — not desk/jobs/lifecycle shells
- Honesty fence: soft-sim only; not surgical device cleared; not live OR; not authors’ system
- Dual goldens that lock both scorers

## Avoid
- Noun-swapping desk templates
- Claiming surgical device clearance or live OR guidance
- Purple / cream-terracotta / broadsheet default looks

## Buyer story
Map the margin without the markers — lock a pack only when marker-free deformable fusion beats marker-based baselines.
