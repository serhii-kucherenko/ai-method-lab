# Drag Wall Studio — what we learned

## Product lesson

Industrial fluid-control soft-sim works when the IA speaks **channels, actuators, sensors, controllers** — not desk nouns. Buyers compare ES closed-loop wall control to open-loop/gradient baselines before locking a pack.

## Method lessons

1. **Distinct IA beats noun-swap.** Mapping contact-arm shells onto drag walls without renaming the domain fails the anti-clone bar.
2. **Dual A/B needs domain verbs.** `es_closed_loop` vs `open_loop_gradient` is clearer than generic “good vs baseline.”
3. **Honesty fence early.** Soft-sim / not live plant / not certified CFD belongs on landing and `/honesty`.
4. **Goldens before polish.** Generate ≥30 dual fixtures from scoreA ≡ scoreB before UI churn.
5. **Commercial surfaces are part of the product.** `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/onboarding`, `/flows` are required, not footnotes.
