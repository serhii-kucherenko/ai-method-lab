# Synth Confidence Studio — what we learned

## Copy this
- Domain nouns: routes, scores, candidates, runs, compares — not jobs/lifecycle/scenario desk shells
- Dual scorers named for the paper pattern: confidence-gated AI retrosynthesis vs naive AI route baseline
- Brand-first landing (Manrope + Source Sans 3; flask ink / confidence green / cool parchment)
- ≥5 named flows on `/flows` with actor, job, steps, success, empty/error
- Soft-sim honesty fence: not wet-lab validated manufacturing routes, not live ELN, not authors’ system

## Refuse this
- Overclaiming manufacturing readiness or live ELN write-back
- Isomorphic desk clones with noun-swapped pages
- Shipping without `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/onboarding`, or multi-flow index
- Same-tick paper pick → smoke scaffold without PM/design pack

## Scoring notes
Maturity passed when 13 pages, 35 features, 30 dual-impl goldens (sc-001…sc-030), and app-up were green. Category practices: goldens, dual compare, scoreboard, versioned route packs, audit/export/webhook/org.
