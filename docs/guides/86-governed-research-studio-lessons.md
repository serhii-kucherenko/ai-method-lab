# Governed Research Studio — lessons

## What we shipped
A soft-sim studio for research leads: study packs, governance gates, research workflows, dual A/B (governed vs ungated agent), scoreboard, and platform must-haves.

## Keep
- Domain IA (`/studies` `/gates` `/workflows`) — not desk/jobs/lifecycle shells
- Honesty fence: soft-sim only; not IRB cleared; not live PHI; not authors’ system
- Dual goldens that lock both scorers

## Avoid
- Noun-swapping enterprise-agent or desk templates
- Claiming IRB clearance or live PHI write-back
- Purple / cream-terracotta / broadsheet default looks

## Buyer story
Research that stays inside the gate — lock a pack only when governed scores beat ungated agent baselines.
