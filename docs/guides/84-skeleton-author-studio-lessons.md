# Skeleton Author Studio — what we learned

Product `skeleton-author-studio` · paper [2607.14579](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14579v1) · authors’ code: none

## Lessons

1. **Domain nouns matter.** Experiences / skeletons / labels / runs is a different product story than jobs/lifecycle desks or tactile-chart noun-swaps.
2. **Scaffold vs linear is the claim.** Dual scorers should reward structure coverage and nav integrity on A, flatten optimism on B — keep leakage risk for WCAG/OEM honesty.
3. **Honesty fence early.** Soft-sim only; not WCAG certified; not live screen-reader OEM; not authors’ system — put it on landing and `/honesty`.
4. **Commercial surfaces are part of the bar.** `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/onboarding`, `/flows` (≥5) prevent “eval desk only” products.
5. **Platform must-haves ship with the climb.** Bearer auth, members, webhook HMAC, audit, export, search, pagination, rate limit.

## Anti-patterns avoided

- Isomorphic desk shells (`/jobs`, `/lifecycle`, `/scenario`)
- Claiming WCAG certification or screen-reader OEM
- Same-tick pick→smoke without depth pack
