# 66 — Hold Match Studio lessons

## What we built
Hold Match Studio models experience-aware hold control for ride-hail matching: match candidates, hold tiers, passenger/driver lanes, timelines, and dual compare vs first-feasible — with `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/onboarding`, and honesty.

## Lessons

1. **Ops money hooks want seats + usage packaging** — Ops / Marketplace / Site license maps cleanly to marketplace matching leads without inventing fake checkout.
2. **Hold IA must not collapse into desk shells** — holds, matches, lanes, timelines beat `/jobs` `/lifecycle` noun-swaps and stay distinct from Video Track clips/probes.
3. **Dual score needs a crisp baseline** — experience-aware (A) vs first-feasible (B) is the falsifiable claim; fluency-style baselines from other products do not transfer.
4. **Soft-sim honesty is the product fence** — never brand as EXHOLD or DiDi production control; paper is research input only; authors’ code none.
5. **Commercial surfaces are sustain gates** — empty `/pricing` `/demo` `/onboarding` stubs fail the comprehensive bar even when goldens are green.
