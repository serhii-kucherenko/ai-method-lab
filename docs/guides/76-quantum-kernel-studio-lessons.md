# Quantum Kernel Studio — what we learned

## Product lesson

Chem-informatics soft-sim works when the IA speaks **molecules, kernels, targets, runs** — not desk nouns. Buyers compare quantum multiple-kernel QSAR to classical kernel baselines before locking a pack.

## Method lessons

1. **Distinct IA beats noun-swap.** Mapping drag-wall shells onto QSAR without renaming the domain fails the anti-clone bar.
2. **Dual A/B needs domain verbs.** `quantum_multi_kernel` vs `classical_kernel` is clearer than generic “good vs baseline.”
3. **Honesty fence early.** Soft-sim / not wet-lab / not live quantum hardware / not Q²SAR belongs on landing and `/honesty`.
4. **Goldens before polish.** Generate ≥30 dual fixtures from scoreA ≡ scoreB before UI churn.
5. **Commercial surfaces are part of the product.** `/pricing`, `/demo`, `/onboarding`, `/flows` are required, not footnotes.
